Internship Benefits

Why Intern with LEON-KNIGHT?

We offer a supportive environment that enables you to build strong skills, real products, and a solid network.

Work on Real Products

No busywork. Interns contribute directly to production products used by clients.

1-on-1 Mentorship

Work alongside senior engineers, designers, and managers who guide your learning path.

Internship Certificate

A certified document acknowledging your successful tenure, responsibilities, and achievements.

Letter of Recommendation

Top performers receive a customized recommendation letter detailing accomplishments.

Portfolio Projects

Build projects you can proudly showcase in your portfolio to secure future opportunities.

Flexible Work Environment

Work remote, onsite, or hybrid. We focus on results, milestones, and output, not hours logged.

Career Growth Guidance

Participate in resume reviews, mock interviews, and technical workshops run by the team.

Industry Networking

Connect with team members, company founders, and partner stakeholders inside our ecosystem.

PPO Opportunities

Outstanding interns receive Pre-Placement Offers to join the team full-time after graduation.
